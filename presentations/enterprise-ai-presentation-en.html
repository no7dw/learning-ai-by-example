09 / 30Enterprise AI / Do not patch old processes
Process Method / AI Original Job

The value limit of AI,
Often byProcess ReconstructDecision.

If AI is simply passed on to the old process, efficiency gains can be re-eatened by redundancy, waiting and double approval.

Common practice / PATCH THE OLD

Insert AI on an old process

  • ObjectiveLet someone do a job faster.
  • ProcessEntry, approval, handover and system boundaries remain unchanged
  • ResultLocally efficient, with the same overall cycle and cost
  • RiskAI becomes a new island, the more complicated it is.
Recommended Practices / REDESIGN THE WORLD

Other Organiser

  • ObjectiveFirst define operational indicators to improve
  • ProcessRemove redundancies, merge duplicates and re-categorize the division of functions
  • ResultLet time, quality or income actually enter business results
  • BorderPeople retain judgement, decision-making, innovation and unusual treatment
Three restructuring issues:Which links can disappear? Which tasks can be given to AI? What high-value judgements do people retain?
